# Brand asset credits

## Artwork

- The Week Dial mark, the Cards app icon and the social preview are original artwork for Mealie Atelier. They are generated by the scripts in `brand/source` of the [mealie-atelier/.github](https://github.com/mealie-atelier/.github/tree/main/brand/source) repository (`build.py` for the marks, `social.sh` for the previews). They contain no photographs and no third-party artwork. The files in this folder are copies of that output.

## Typeface

- The text in the social preview is set in [Bricolage Grotesque](https://github.com/ateliertriay/bricolage), licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The font file and the licence text are in `brand/source/font/` of the same `.github` repository (`BricolageGrotesque.ttf`, `OFL.txt`).
