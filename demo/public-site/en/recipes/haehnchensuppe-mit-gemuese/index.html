Chicken and vegetable soup

Chicken, carrots and celery simmer in a clear broth. Small pasta turns the soup into a satisfying meal.

15 minutes prep · 35 minutes cooking · 4 servings

Photo by Babs Gorniak · Unsplash

Ingredients

4 servings

  • 450 g chicken breast
  • 1.2 l chicken stock
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 onion
  • 120 g small soup pasta
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Method

  1. 1. Start the stock

    Poach the chicken gently

    Bring the stock, whole chicken breast and halved onion to a gentle simmer. Cover and cook for 15 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.

  2. 2. Cook the vegetables

    Add the carrots and celery to the stock

    Remove the chicken and let it rest briefly. Add the carrots and celery to the stock and simmer for 8 minutes. Shred the chicken with two forks.

  3. 3. Finish the noodles

    Serve hot with parsley

    Add the pasta to the soup and cook according to the packet. Return the chicken to the pot, season with salt and pepper, and serve with parsley.
