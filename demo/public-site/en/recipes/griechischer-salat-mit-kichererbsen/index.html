Greek chickpea salad

Crisp cucumber, tomatoes, chickpeas and feta meet a lemon and oregano dressing for a fresh main-course salad.

20 minutes prep · 0 minutes cooking · 4 servings

Photo by Elena Leya · Unsplash

Ingredients

4 servings

  • 400 g drained chickpeas
  • 1 cucumber
  • 250 g cherry tomatoes
  • 1 small red onion
  • 150 g feta
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp dried oregano

Method

  1. 1. Prepare the vegetables

    Cut the cucumber, tomatoes and onion

    Rinse and drain the chickpeas well. Dice the cucumber, halve the tomatoes and thinly slice the red onion.

  2. 2. Mix the dressing

    Bring lemon and oregano together

    Whisk the olive oil with the lemon juice, oregano, a pinch of salt and black pepper.

  3. 3. Assemble the salad

    Crumble over the feta and serve

    Toss the chickpeas and vegetables with the dressing. Crumble feta over the top and serve with olives and fresh parsley if you like.
