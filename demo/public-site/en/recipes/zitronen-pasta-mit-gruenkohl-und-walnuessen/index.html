Lemon pasta with kale and walnuts

Lemon, kale and toasted walnuts bring freshness, crunch and depth to this quick pasta, with no dairy needed.

10 minutes prep · 15 minutes cooking · 4 servings

Photo by Sebastian Coman Photography · Unsplash

Ingredients

4 servings

  • 350 g spaghetti
  • 120 g stemmed kale
  • 1 unwaxed lemon
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 50 g roughly chopped walnuts
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 150 ml pasta water

Method

  1. 1. Cook the pasta

    Reserve some pasta water

    Cook the spaghetti in well-salted water until al dente. Scoop out 150 ml pasta water before draining.

  2. 2. Toast the kale and walnuts

    Keep the garlic from browning

    Toast the walnuts in a large dry pan and set aside. Warm the olive oil, finely grate in the garlic and cook gently for 30 seconds. Add the kale and cook for 3–4 minutes until wilted.

  3. 3. Emulsify the sauce

    Finish with lemon zest and pepper

    Toss the spaghetti with the kale, lemon zest, two tablespoons of lemon juice and a little pasta water. Add more pasta water until glossy. Scatter over the walnuts.
