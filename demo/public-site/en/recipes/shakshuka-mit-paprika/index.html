Shakshuka with peppers

Eggs simmer in a warmly spiced tomato and pepper sauce. Finish with parsley and serve with bread for an easy supper.

15 minutes prep · 25 minutes cooking · 4 servings

Photo by Toa Heftiba · Unsplash

Ingredients

4 servings

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 4 slices sourdough bread

Method

  1. 1. Soften the vegetables

    Cook the onion and pepper

    Heat the olive oil in a large ovenproof pan. Cook the onion and bell pepper over medium heat for 7–8 minutes until soft. Stir in the garlic, cumin and paprika and cook for 30 seconds.

  2. 2. Simmer the sauce

    Reduce the tomatoes

    Add the tomatoes and their juices, season with salt and pepper, and simmer uncovered for 10–12 minutes until thick. Make four wells in the sauce.

  3. 3. Set the eggs

    Finish with parsley and bread

    Crack an egg into each well. Cover and cook for 5–7 minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks are still soft. Scatter over the parsley and serve with toasted sourdough.
